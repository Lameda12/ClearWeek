# ClearWeek 2 minute demo checklist

**Alamedin Sabit** · Open demo, about 2 minutes. The exact wording to say is in the [recording script](demo-script.md). This page is the checklist around it.

I record the open demo at clearweek-woad.vercel.app. It has no sign-in and no AI calls. The draft is a prepared example.

## Before recording

- [ ] Open a **new private (incognito) window** and go to clearweek-woad.vercel.app. The open demo saves its follow-up in the browser, and a normal reload does not clear it. A private window always starts clean.
- [ ] Check the badge at the top right says **Open demo**, and the label above the heading says **Sample data**. The page title is "Chloe’s Friday schedule".
- [ ] Check that **Prepare draft** is visible and the **Follow-up tasks** section says "No follow-up tasks yet." If a task is already saved, the Prepare draft button is hidden. Close the window and open a new private one.
- [ ] Close all other tabs. Hide the bookmarks bar. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pops up.
- [ ] Check that no email, account name or API key is visible anywhere on screen, including the browser toolbar and profile icon. The open demo itself shows none.
- [ ] Zoom the browser to 110 to 125% so the text reads on a phone. Check that the conflict card and the draft still fit on screen without much scrolling.
- [ ] Have the recording script open on a second screen, with a 2 minute timer.
- [ ] Record a 5 second test clip and play it back to check the audio.

## The take, about 2 minutes

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:15 | The page top: **Chloe’s Friday schedule**. | Who Chloe is, and what went wrong: her Friday, October 23 swap was declined, and she is still scheduled. |
| 0:15 to 0:40 | The **Day schedule** list: **Team bus to Saint John** (2:00 PM to 6:00 PM) and **Harbour Foods shift** (4:00 PM to 10:00 PM). Then the **Schedule conflict** card, which shows **2 hours overlap, 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM**. | The bus and the shift overlap by two hours, from 4 to 6 PM. Plain code does the math, with no AI. |
| 0:40 to 1:00 | Click **View source evidence**. Point to each item and its source. Point to **Work calendar · view only** on the shift. | Each fact shows where it came from. The work calendar is view only, so she cannot change the shift there. Say "Sample data" aloud: this is sample data from Chloe’s screenshots, not real. |
| 1:00 to 1:30 | Click **Prepare draft**. Point to the label **Open demo draft** and read the message to Dana asking about approved coverage. Change one small thing in the **Draft message** box. The label changes to **Edited by you**. | This is a prepared example, not written by AI. She can edit it, so she stays in control. |
| 1:30 to 1:50 | Click **Review draft**, then **Approve & create task**. Click **View saved follow-up** and show **Follow-up tasks**. Reload the page. Show the task is still there and the conflict still says **Conflict open**. | Approving saves a follow-up in ClearWeek. After a reload it is still here, and the conflict is still open. |
| 1:50 to 2:00 | Scroll back to the top, or stay on **Conflict open**. | What ClearWeek does not do: no live calendar, no sending messages, no access to the employer’s schedule. Chloe still contacts Dana herself. |

Do not click **Record a resolution** in the recording. Do not say Gemini wrote the draft.
