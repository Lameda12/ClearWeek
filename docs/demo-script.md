# ClearWeek 90-second demo

Open the offline demo in a fresh browser profile. Start at the top of the page.

| Time | Click | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 s | Point to **Historical sample** and the Friday date. | I’m Alamedin Sabit, and I built ClearWeek for Chloe. She’s a nursing student, a swimmer, and a cashier. This is her October 23 sample schedule. |
| 15–32 s | Click **View source evidence** and point to the bus, shift and swap. | Her bus runs from two to six. Her shift starts at four. That’s a two-hour overlap. The swap was declined, and the work calendar says the shift can’t be edited there. |
| 32–58 s | Click **Prepare draft**. In **Draft message**, add “Could we talk before the bus leaves?” before the sign-off. Click **Review draft**. | This offline demo prepares an example note to Dana, the store manager. I can edit it, then review the exact version I want to save. |
| 58–74 s | Click **Approve & create task**, then **View saved follow-up**. | ClearWeek saves the draft with a follow-up task. I still have to talk to Dana. The task is saved here in ClearWeek. |
| 74–90 s | Reload. Point to the saved task and **Action history**, then scroll to **Conflict open**. | The task survives a reload. The conflict stays open until I have a confirmed outcome and record it here. |

## Likely questions

1. **What part uses AI?** Gemini reads the sample evidence in private mode and chooses how to frame the message. ClearWeek calculates the overlap from the calendar times.
2. **Does it send the message?** I can review and save the draft as a task. I would contact Dana myself.
3. **What if I edit after reviewing?** ClearWeek asks me to review the new version before I can approve it.
4. **Why is the conflict still open?** A follow-up is a reminder to act. I need supporting information, like confirmation from Dana, before I record a resolution.
5. **What does the demo cost?** The offline demo makes no model calls. Private mode is designed to start with a free-tier key, with a limit on new calls.

## Cue card

- **Problem** — Bus and shift overlap. Swap declined.
- **Action** — Edit, review, approve a follow-up.
- **Evidence** — Show the calendar and notice. Reload and point to the open conflict.
